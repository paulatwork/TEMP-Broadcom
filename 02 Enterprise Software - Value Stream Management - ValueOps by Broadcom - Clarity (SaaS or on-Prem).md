## Broadcom Prdouct Summary Description (This is important to get right)

Clarity by Broadcom (formerly CA Clarity PPM / CA PPM) is a Strategic Portfolio Management (SPM) platform connecting enterprise strategy, funding and execution. It provides investment and resource management, financial planning, roadmapping, portfolio governance and value stream execution tracking, and integrates with Rally, ConnectALL and Insights within the ValueOps suite. Recent releases have added the Vaia AI layer for forecasting and scenario planning, together with newer modules for AI investment governance and infrastructure portfolio management.
