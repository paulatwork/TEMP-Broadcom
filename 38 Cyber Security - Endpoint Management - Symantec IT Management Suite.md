Automates operating system and application deployment, patch management, configuration management, and hardware/software asset and inventory tracking across large, heterogeneous device estates from a centralised management console (formerly marketed as Altiris).

## Analyst Cautions and Industry Findings - Related to the Legacy Broadcom Product. Include newest findings on Broadcom exist strategies

Symantec IT Management Suite (Client Management) is rated 4.3 out of 5 on Gartner Peer Insights from 60 reviews, and is well regarded for centralised endpoint management, software deployment, inventory and patch management from a single console. Reviewers specifically flag Broadcom's account prioritisation as a direct issue: one reviewer states 'Lack of support for non-strategic customers is a big issue,' which directly corroborates Broadcom's documented core-account strategy for this specific product. Other reviewers cite dependency on an ageing .NET Framework architecture and a shrinking pool of skilled implementation partners as ongoing risks.

No independent Gartner Magic Quadrant or Forrester Wave coverage specific to this product was identified; Gartner's structured market coverage for endpoint/unified endpoint management does not appear to include a current ranked entry for Symantec/Altiris.

IT operations teams are decommissioning Altiris/IT Management Suite in favour of modern cloud-managed Unified Endpoint Management (UEM) and systems-management platforms, principally Microsoft Intune, IBM MaaS360, Tanium, and Red Hat Satellite for Linux estates.

Sources: Gartner Peer Insights, 'Symantec Client Management Suite Reviews & Ratings' (gartner.com/reviews).
